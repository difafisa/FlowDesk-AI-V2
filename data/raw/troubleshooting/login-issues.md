# Login Troubleshooting

## 1. Overview

This guide helps customers and support agents troubleshoot common problems when signing in to FlowDesk.

If you cannot log in, first verify that your email address and password are correct. If the credentials are correct, the problem may be related to a temporary account lock, browser session, expired session, or an Enterprise SSO configuration.

Follow the troubleshooting procedure that best matches the symptoms you are experiencing.

---

## 2. Common Login Problems

### Incorrect Email or Password

The most common cause of login failure is an incorrect email address or password.

**Common symptoms:**

- FlowDesk reports that the credentials are invalid.
- The login form returns to the sign-in page after submission.
- You cannot sign in even though the FlowDesk account exists.

**Possible causes:**

- The email address contains a typing error.
- The wrong email address is being used.
- The password was entered incorrectly.
- The password was recently changed and the previous password is still being used.

---

### Invalid Credentials Error

An invalid credentials message indicates that FlowDesk could not authenticate the supplied sign-in information.

Before retrying repeatedly, carefully check the email address and password.

> **Note:** Passwords are case-sensitive. Check whether Caps Lock or another keyboard setting is affecting the password you enter.

---

### Forgotten Password

If you do not remember your FlowDesk password, use the password-reset process instead of repeatedly attempting to sign in.

Password reset is available from the FlowDesk login page.

---

### Account Temporarily Locked

Multiple unsuccessful login attempts may temporarily lock an account as a security precaution.

**Common symptoms:**

- Login attempts continue to fail even after the credentials have been corrected.
- FlowDesk indicates that the account is temporarily unavailable for sign-in.
- The user recently made several unsuccessful login attempts.

**Recommended action:**

Stop attempting to sign in and follow the instructions displayed by FlowDesk. If access remains unavailable after the temporary restriction has cleared, contact FlowDesk support.

---

### Login Page Does Not Load Correctly

The login page may fail to load completely or may display incorrectly.

**Possible causes include:**

- Temporary network connectivity problems.
- An outdated browser session.
- Cached browser data.
- Browser extensions interfering with page behavior.
- A temporary browser problem.

---

### Browser-Related Login Problems

A browser may prevent successful login when cached data or an existing session becomes inconsistent with the current FlowDesk login state.

Symptoms can include:

- Buttons not responding.
- The login form not submitting.
- The page repeatedly refreshing.
- Unexpected redirects to the login page.

---

### Session Expiration

FlowDesk sessions may expire after a period of inactivity or when the existing session is no longer valid.

When this happens, you may be returned to the login page.

Sign in again using your FlowDesk credentials.

---

### Outdated Browser Sessions

An old browser session can interfere with a new login attempt, particularly when multiple FlowDesk tabs have been open for an extended period.

Closing the affected FlowDesk tabs and starting a new browser session can resolve the problem.

---

## 3. Troubleshooting Procedures

### 3.1 Incorrect Email or Password

**Symptom:** FlowDesk reports invalid credentials or does not allow you to sign in.

**Possible cause:** The email address or password is incorrect.

**Recommended steps:**

1. Confirm that you are using the email address associated with your FlowDesk account.
2. Check the email address for spelling errors or accidental spaces.
3. Re-enter your password carefully.
4. Check whether Caps Lock is enabled.
5. If you recently changed your password, use the new password.
6. Attempt to sign in again.

**Expected result:** You should be able to access your FlowDesk workspace when the correct credentials are provided.

If you still cannot sign in, use the password-reset procedure.

---

### 3.2 Forgotten Password

**Symptom:** You cannot remember the password associated with your FlowDesk account.

**Possible cause:** The password is unknown or no longer available.

**Recommended steps:**

1. Open the FlowDesk login page.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the email address associated with your FlowDesk account.
4. Submit the password-reset request.
5. Check the inbox associated with the account.
6. Open the password-reset email from FlowDesk.
7. Follow the reset link and create a new password.
8. Return to the login page and sign in using the new password.

**Expected result:** You should be able to sign in using the newly created password.

---

### 3.3 Account Temporarily Locked

**Symptom:** Login continues to fail after several unsuccessful attempts, or FlowDesk indicates that the account is temporarily locked.

**Possible cause:** Multiple failed login attempts triggered a temporary security restriction.

**Recommended steps:**

1. Stop submitting additional login attempts.
2. Review any instructions displayed by FlowDesk.
3. Wait for the temporary restriction to clear.
4. Confirm that you know the correct email address and password.
5. Attempt to sign in again.
6. If you do not remember the password, use the password-reset process instead of repeatedly trying different passwords.

**Expected result:** The account should become available for login after the temporary restriction has cleared.

If the account remains inaccessible, contact FlowDesk support.

---

### 3.4 Login Page Not Loading

**Symptom:** The FlowDesk login page is blank, incomplete, or fails to respond.

**Possible cause:** A browser, network, or cached-session problem.

**Recommended steps:**

1. Refresh the login page.
2. Check that your internet connection is working.
3. Close unnecessary FlowDesk browser tabs.
4. Close and reopen the browser.
5. Try opening FlowDesk in a private or incognito window.
6. If the problem persists, clear the browser cache and cookies associated with FlowDesk.
7. Try again using a supported modern browser.

**Expected result:** The FlowDesk login page should load normally and allow you to submit your credentials.

---

### 3.5 Browser Session Problems

**Symptom:** The login form does not submit, repeatedly reloads, or behaves unexpectedly.

**Possible cause:** Corrupted or outdated browser session data.

**Recommended steps:**

1. Close all open FlowDesk tabs.
2. Open a new browser window.
3. Navigate to the FlowDesk login page.
4. Try signing in again.
5. If the problem continues, open FlowDesk in a private or incognito window.
6. If the private window works correctly, clear the FlowDesk-related browser cache and cookies in the normal browser session.
7. Reopen the login page and sign in again.

**Expected result:** The login process should work normally after the outdated session data has been removed.

---

### 3.6 Session Expiration

**Symptom:** You were previously signed in but are now viewing the login page.

**Possible cause:** Your FlowDesk session has expired or is no longer valid.

**Recommended steps:**

1. Confirm that you are on the FlowDesk login page.
2. Enter your FlowDesk email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. If the login page continues to appear after a successful sign-in attempt, close the browser tab.
5. Open a new FlowDesk session and sign in again.
6. If necessary, repeat the process using a private or incognito browser window.

**Expected result:** A new valid session should be established and you should return to your FlowDesk workspace.

---

## 4. Password Reset

### Starting a Password Reset

You can start a password reset from the FlowDesk login page.

1. Open the FlowDesk login page.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the email address associated with your FlowDesk account.
4. Submit the request.
5. Check the email inbox associated with the account.
6. Open the password-reset email.
7. Follow the link provided in the message.
8. Create a new password.
9. Return to the login page and sign in with the new password.

The password-reset process requires access to the email address associated with the FlowDesk account.

### If the Reset Email Does Not Arrive

If you requested a password reset but did not receive an email:

1. Check your spam or junk-mail folder.
2. Verify that you entered the correct email address.
3. Check whether the message was filtered or automatically moved by your email provider.
4. Wait a few minutes and submit another password-reset request if necessary.
5. If the email still does not arrive, contact FlowDesk support.

> **Note:** Do not repeatedly submit password-reset requests in rapid succession. Use the most recent valid reset email when multiple messages have been requested.

### If the Reset Link Has Expired

Password-reset links are temporary for security reasons.

If a reset link has expired:

1. Return to the FlowDesk login page.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your account email address.
4. Submit a new password-reset request.
5. Open the newest reset email.
6. Follow the new reset link and complete the password reset.

If a newly generated reset link also fails, contact FlowDesk support.

## 5. Browser and Session Troubleshooting

Browser and session problems can often be resolved without changing account settings.

Try the following steps in order:

1. **Refresh the page.**
   Reload the FlowDesk login page and try again.

2. **Close old FlowDesk tabs.**
   Close previously opened FlowDesk tabs to remove outdated page sessions.

3. **Restart the browser.**
   Close and reopen your browser before attempting another login.

4. **Use a private or incognito window.**
   This can help determine whether cached data or existing cookies are causing the problem.

5. **Clear FlowDesk-related cache and cookies.**
   Remove cached data and cookies associated with FlowDesk if the private-window test works but the normal browser session does not.

6. **Use a supported modern browser.**
   Make sure your browser is up to date. If possible, test the login using another modern browser.

7. **Sign in again after session expiration.**
   If FlowDesk returns you to the login page after inactivity, sign in again to establish a new session.

> **Note:** Clearing browser data may sign you out of websites that use the affected cookies. Make sure you know the credentials required to sign in again.

## 6. Enterprise SSO Considerations

SSO is available **only to Enterprise customers**.

Enterprise customers whose organization uses FlowDesk SSO may experience login problems that are different from standard email-and-password authentication.

### Common Enterprise SSO Symptoms

- The user cannot complete the organization's SSO sign-in flow.
- The user is redirected back to the login page.
- The organization has recently changed its SSO configuration.
- Other members of the organization can sign in, but a particular user cannot.

### Recommended Steps

1. Confirm that your organization uses SSO for FlowDesk.
2. Make sure you are using the organization's configured SSO sign-in method.
3. Close existing FlowDesk tabs and start a new browser session.
4. Try signing in using a private or incognito window.
5. If the problem persists for multiple users, contact your organization's FlowDesk administrator.
6. If the administrator cannot resolve the issue, contact FlowDesk support.

> **Important:** SSO is an Enterprise-only feature. Free and Pro customers should use the standard FlowDesk login process rather than attempting to troubleshoot an SSO configuration.

---

## 7. When to Contact Support

Contact FlowDesk support when the issue cannot be resolved using the troubleshooting procedures in this guide.

Support should be contacted in particular when:

- Login continues to fail after verifying the credentials and completing browser troubleshooting.
- The account remains temporarily locked after the expected restriction period.
- You suspect that someone else may have accessed or attempted to access your account.
- Password-reset emails do not arrive after verifying the account email address and checking spam or junk folders.
- Password-reset links repeatedly fail or cannot be used.
- An Enterprise SSO configuration is preventing users from accessing FlowDesk.
- Multiple users in the same Enterprise organization experience the same SSO login problem.

When contacting support, provide a description of the problem, the steps already attempted, and any error message displayed by FlowDesk.

> **Security warning:** Never include your password or other authentication secrets in a support request.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I log in even though I entered my password correctly?

First verify that you are using the correct FlowDesk account email address and that the password is entered exactly as intended. If the problem continues, try the browser and session troubleshooting steps. If necessary, reset your password.

### What should I do if I forget my FlowDesk password?

Select **Forgot password?** on the FlowDesk login page and enter the email address associated with your account. Follow the password-reset email to create a new password.

### Why didn't I receive my password-reset email?

Check your spam or junk-mail folder and verify that you entered the correct account email address. If the message still does not arrive, submit another reset request. Persistent problems should be reported to FlowDesk support.

### What should I do if my account is temporarily locked?

Stop making additional login attempts and follow any instructions displayed by FlowDesk. Wait for the temporary restriction to clear before trying again. If the account remains inaccessible, contact FlowDesk support.

### Why does FlowDesk keep sending me back to the login page?

Your session may have expired, or an outdated browser session may be interfering with authentication. Close old FlowDesk tabs, restart the browser, and sign in again. A private or incognito window can also help identify browser-session problems.

### Should I clear my browser cookies if FlowDesk will not let me log in?

Clearing FlowDesk-related cache and cookies can resolve outdated browser-session problems. Before doing so, make sure you know the credentials required to sign in again.

### Does FlowDesk support SSO?

Yes. SSO is available only to **Enterprise** customers. Free and Pro customers do not have access to FlowDesk SSO.

### What should Enterprise customers do if SSO login fails?

Confirm that the organization's SSO configuration is correct and try signing in from a fresh or private browser session. If the problem continues, contact the organization's FlowDesk administrator. Persistent configuration problems should be escalated to FlowDesk support.
