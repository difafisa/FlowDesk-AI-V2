# FlowDesk Data Retention Policy

## 1. Data Retention Policy Overview

FlowDesk retains customer support data after an account is terminated to support the applicable data lifecycle and account closure processes.

The **standard retention period is 12 months after account termination**.

Enterprise customers may request a custom retention period when supported by their applicable contract. A custom Enterprise retention period must be governed by that contract.

> **Important:** Account cancellation does not mean that customer support data is deleted immediately.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to customer support data associated with FlowDesk support functionality.

It describes:

- The standard retention period.
- What happens to customer support data after account termination.
- How Enterprise contractual retention arrangements work.
- The distinction between cancellation, termination, retention, and eventual deletion.

This policy does not state that FlowDesk retains every type of customer data indefinitely.

## 3. Types of Data Covered

Customer support data covered by this policy may include information associated with FlowDesk support functionality, such as:

- **Tickets** — support tickets and their associated information.
- **Customer profiles** — customer information maintained for support purposes.
- **Ticket history** — historical information associated with support tickets.
- **Support-related metadata** — metadata associated with support activities.
- **Automation-related records** — records associated with support automation where applicable.
- **Analytics-related support records** — support records used for analytics where applicable.

The specific data retained may depend on the information associated with the customer's use of FlowDesk.

## 4. Standard Retention Period

The standard FlowDesk retention period for customer support data is:

**12 months after account termination.**

Unless an applicable Enterprise contractual arrangement specifies a different retention period, the 12-month period applies.

| Account situation                              | Standard retention treatment                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Active account                                 | Customer support data is maintained as part of the account's ongoing use of FlowDesk |
| Account cancelled                              | Cancellation does not immediately delete customer support data                       |
| Account terminated                             | Customer support data is retained for 12 months                                      |
| Enterprise with valid custom contractual terms | Retention follows the applicable contract                                            |

## 5. Retention After Account Termination

Account termination begins the standard retention period for customer support data.

After an account is terminated:

1. The account is no longer active.
2. Customer support data is retained for the applicable retention period.
3. Under the standard policy, the data is retained for 12 months after termination.
4. After the applicable retention period, the data may proceed through the applicable deletion lifecycle.

The standard policy does **not** provide for immediate deletion when an account is terminated.

## 6. Enterprise Custom Retention

Enterprise customers may request custom retention periods subject to their contract.

A custom retention period is not automatically provided to every Enterprise customer.

For a custom retention arrangement to apply:

- The customer must have an applicable contractual arrangement with FlowDesk.
- The retention period must be governed by that contract.
- The contractual terms determine the applicable retention period for the covered data.

Where no valid Enterprise contractual arrangement specifies a different period, the standard **12-month retention period** applies.

> **Note:** Customers should review their Enterprise agreement or contact FlowDesk Support if they need confirmation of their applicable retention period.

## 7. Data Deletion and Lifecycle

FlowDesk distinguishes between account cancellation, account termination, data retention, and eventual deletion.

### Account Cancellation

Cancellation is the action of ending an active subscription or account according to the applicable subscription terms.

Cancellation does not mean that customer support data is immediately deleted.

### Account Termination

Account termination marks the point after which the account is no longer active and the applicable post-termination retention period begins.

### Data Retention

During the standard post-termination retention period, customer support data is retained for **12 months after account termination**.

### Eventual Deletion

Once the applicable retention period has been reached, customer support data may proceed through the applicable deletion lifecycle.

The standard policy does not provide for indefinite retention.

### Lifecycle Summary

```text
Active Account
      |
      v

      |
      v
Account Termination
      |
      v
12-Month Standard Retention Period
      |
      v
Applicable Data Deletion Lifecycle
```

For Enterprise customers with a valid custom contractual retention period, the applicable contract governs the retention period instead of the standard period.

## 8. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for managing their information appropriately while using FlowDesk.

Customers should:

- Maintain information that is necessary for their support operations.
- Review their data before cancelling or terminating an account.
- Consider applicable retention requirements when managing their FlowDesk account.
- Review their Enterprise contract if a custom retention period applies.
- Contact FlowDesk Support when they need clarification about the applicable retention period.

Customers should not assume that cancellation results in immediate deletion of customer support data.

## 9. Data Retention and Account Cancellation

Cancellation and termination are not the same as immediate data deletion.

When an account is cancelled, customers should expect the applicable account termination process to occur according to their subscription terms. Following account termination, customer support data remains subject to the applicable retention policy.

Under the standard policy:

**Customer support data is retained for 12 months after account termination.**

Therefore:

- Cancelling an account does not immediately delete customer support data.
- Account termination starts the applicable post-termination retention period.
- The standard retention period is 12 months.
- Enterprise customers may have a different retention period if their applicable contract specifies one.
- The standard policy does not provide for indefinite retention.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does FlowDesk retain support data?

The standard retention period is **12 months after account termination**.

### What happens to my data after I cancel?

Cancellation does not immediately delete customer support data. After the account is terminated, the data remains subject to the applicable retention period. Under the standard policy, customer support data is retained for 12 months after termination.

### Is data deleted immediately after account termination?

No. The standard policy provides for a **12-month retention period after account termination**. Data may proceed through the applicable deletion lifecycle after the retention period.

### Can Enterprise customers request a different retention period?

Yes. Enterprise customers may request custom retention periods subject to their contract.

### Does every Enterprise customer automatically receive a custom retention period?

No. A custom retention period applies only where it is supported by the customer's applicable contract.

### Does the 12-month period apply to Enterprise customers?

Yes, unless a valid Enterprise contractual arrangement specifies a different retention period. Without such an arrangement, the standard 12-month retention period applies.

### Does cancelling my account delete my support tickets immediately?

No. Account cancellation does not result in immediate deletion of customer support data. The standard policy retains the data for 12 months after account termination.

### What types of support data are covered?

Covered customer support data may include tickets, customer profiles, ticket history, support-related metadata, automation-related records where applicable, and analytics-related support records where applicable.

### Does FlowDesk retain all customer data indefinitely?

No. This policy does not provide for indefinite retention. The standard customer support data retention period is 12 months after account termination, subject to applicable Enterprise contractual arrangements.

### How can I find out whether a custom Enterprise retention period applies to my account?

Review the applicable Enterprise contract or contact FlowDesk Support for clarification.
