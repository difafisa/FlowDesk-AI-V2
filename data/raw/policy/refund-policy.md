# FlowDesk Refund Policy

## 1. Refund Policy Overview

FlowDesk provides a refund process for customers who are dissatisfied with an initial subscription purchase. Customers may request a refund within **14 days of the initial subscription purchase**.

A refund request does not automatically guarantee approval. Each request is reviewed by FlowDesk Support according to this policy.

This policy applies to FlowDesk subscriptions, including Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans, subject to any applicable contractual terms for Enterprise customers.

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

Customers may request a refund when:

- The request is made within 14 days of the initial subscription purchase.
- The request relates to the initial subscription purchase.
- The customer provides sufficient information for FlowDesk Support to identify and review the subscription.

Submitting a request within the eligible period does not guarantee that a refund will be approved. FlowDesk Support reviews requests individually according to the applicable policy.

## 3. 14-Day Refund Window

The refund request period is **14 days from the date of the initial subscription purchase**.

The 14-day period applies specifically to the initial subscription purchase. It does not automatically reset when a subscription renews.

**Note:** Customers should submit refund requests as soon as possible if they believe they are eligible.

## 4. Initial Subscription Purchase

The initial subscription purchase is the first paid subscription transaction for the applicable FlowDesk subscription.

Customers may request a refund for an initial subscription purchase within the 14-day refund window.

For example, if a customer purchases a Pro subscription on September 1, the customer may submit a refund request during the applicable 14-day period following that initial purchase.

## 5. Subscription Renewals

Renewal payments are **generally non-refundable**.

The 14-day refund period applies to the initial subscription purchase and does not create a new 14-day refund period for each renewal.

In exceptional circumstances, FlowDesk Support may approve a refund for a renewal payment. Such approval is determined on a case-by-case basis.

## 6. Refund Exceptions

A refund request is subject to review and is not automatically approved.

FlowDesk Support may consider relevant circumstances when reviewing a request, including:

- Whether the request concerns an initial subscription purchase.
- Whether the request was submitted within the applicable 14-day window.
- Whether the payment was a subscription renewal.
- Any applicable contractual terms.

Renewal payments remain generally non-refundable unless otherwise approved by FlowDesk Support.

## 7. How to Request a Refund

Customers can submit a refund request to FlowDesk Support.

Follow these steps:

1. Contact FlowDesk Support and state that you are requesting a subscription refund.
2. Identify the FlowDesk account associated with the subscription.
3. Provide the relevant purchase information.
4. Explain the reason for the refund request.
5. Submit the request for review.
6. Wait for FlowDesk Support to review the request and communicate the outcome.

**Note:** Submit the request within 14 days of the initial subscription purchase if you want the request considered under the standard initial-purchase refund window.

## 8. Information Required for a Refund Request

To help FlowDesk Support review a request, customers should provide:

- Account information associated with the subscription.
- Subscription plan.
- Date of the initial subscription purchase.
- Relevant payment or transaction information.
- Reason for requesting the refund.

Providing complete information can help FlowDesk Support identify the applicable subscription and review the request efficiently.

## 9. Refund Review Process

FlowDesk Support reviews refund requests according to this policy.

During the review, Support may determine:

- Whether the request concerns the initial subscription purchase.
- Whether it was submitted within the 14-day refund window.
- Whether the payment was a renewal.
- Whether any applicable Enterprise contractual terms affect the request.

A refund request is **not automatically approved** simply because it was submitted within 14 days. FlowDesk Support makes the final determination based on the applicable policy and circumstances of the request.

## 10. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, FlowDesk Support will provide information about the refund processing as part of the resolution of the request.

The refund outcome is communicated to the customer after the request has been reviewed.

FlowDesk does not specify a fixed refund percentage in this policy.

## 11. Enterprise Customers

Enterprise customers may have contractual terms that affect billing and refunds.

Where an Enterprise agreement contains applicable billing or refund provisions, those contractual terms may be considered during the refund review. Enterprise-specific terms must remain consistent with the FlowDesk global refund policy.

Enterprise customers should contact FlowDesk Support if they need clarification about how their contractual terms apply to a refund request.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a refund after 14 days?

The standard refund window for an initial subscription purchase is 14 days. Requests submitted after this period are not covered by the standard 14-day refund window and are subject to FlowDesk Support review.

### Are renewal payments refundable?

Renewal payments are generally non-refundable. FlowDesk Support may approve an exception in certain circumstances.

### Does the refund policy apply to all plans?

The refund policy applies to FlowDesk subscriptions, including Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans. Enterprise customers may also have contractual terms that affect billing and refunds.

### How do I request a refund?

Contact FlowDesk Support and provide your account information, subscription plan, initial purchase date, relevant payment information, and reason for the request.

### Is a refund automatic?

No. Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval. FlowDesk Support reviews each request according to the applicable policy.

### Does the 14-day period restart when my subscription renews?

No. The 14-day refund period applies to the initial subscription purchase. It does not automatically restart for subscription renewals.

### What happens after I submit my request?

FlowDesk Support reviews the request, determines whether it meets the applicable policy requirements, and communicates the outcome to you.

### Example: I purchased Pro 10 days ago and want a refund. Am I within the refund window?

Yes. A request made 10 days after the initial Pro subscription purchase falls within the 14-day refund window. However, the request is still subject to review and approval by FlowDesk Support.
