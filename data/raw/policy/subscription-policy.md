# FlowDesk Subscription Policy

## 1. Subscription Policy Overview

FlowDesk offers subscription tiers designed for different team sizes and support requirements. Customers can use the **Free**, **Pro**, or **Enterprise** subscription tier.

Each plan provides a defined set of product capabilities and usage limits. Pro and Enterprise customers are billed monthly.

### Plan Comparison

| Feature            |        Free |          Pro | Enterprise |
| ------------------ | ----------: | -----------: | ---------: |
| Maximum agents     |           3 |           20 |  Unlimited |
| Maximum tickets    | 1,000/month | 10,000/month |  Unlimited |
| API access         |          No |          Yes |        Yes |
| Webhooks           |          No |          Yes |        Yes |
| Advanced analytics |          No |          Yes |        Yes |
| SSO                |          No |           No |        Yes |
| SLA                |          No |           No |        Yes |

## 2. Available Subscription Plans

FlowDesk provides three subscription tiers:

- **Free** — For teams that need core FlowDesk capabilities within defined usage limits.
- **Pro** — For teams that need higher usage limits, API access, webhooks, and advanced analytics.
- **Enterprise** — For organizations requiring unlimited agents and tickets, SSO, SLA, and the capabilities available on Pro.

## 3. Free Plan

The Free plan is a FlowDesk subscription tier with the following limits and capabilities:

- Maximum of 3 agents.
- Maximum of 1,000 tickets per month.
- API access is unavailable.
- Webhooks are unavailable.
- Advanced analytics are unavailable.
- SSO is unavailable.
- SLA is unavailable.

Free customers can use FlowDesk within these plan limits.

## 4. Pro Plan

The Pro plan is designed for teams requiring greater capacity and additional product capabilities.

Pro includes:

- Maximum of 20 agents.
- Maximum of 10,000 tickets per month.
- API access.
- Webhooks.
- Advanced analytics.

Pro does not include:

- SSO.
- SLA.

Pro customers are billed monthly.

## 5. Enterprise Plan

The Enterprise plan provides the highest level of subscription capacity and feature availability.

Enterprise includes:

- Unlimited agents.
- Unlimited tickets.
- API access.
- Webhooks.
- Advanced analytics.
- SSO.
- SLA.

Enterprise customers are billed monthly.

**Note:** Enterprise customers may have additional contractual terms that apply to their subscription.

## 6. Subscription Billing

FlowDesk subscriptions are available as Free, Pro, and Enterprise tiers.

Billing depends on the selected subscription:

| Subscription | Billing                                                         |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free         | Subscription tier with no monthly billing requirement specified |
| Pro          | Monthly                                                         |
| Enterprise   | Monthly                                                         |

FlowDesk does not specify annual billing in this subscription policy.

## 7. Monthly Billing

Pro and Enterprise subscriptions are billed monthly.

Monthly billing means that the subscription is charged on a recurring monthly basis while the subscription remains active.

Customers should review their subscription status and billing information before making plan changes or cancelling their subscription.

## 8. Upgrading a Subscription

Customers can move to a higher subscription tier when they need additional capacity or features.

### Upgrade from Free to Pro

To upgrade from Free to Pro:

1. Open the subscription or billing settings for the FlowDesk account.
2. Select **Pro** as the new subscription tier.
3. Review the plan's features and limits.
4. Confirm the subscription change.
5. Verify that the account now shows Pro as its active subscription.

The Pro plan provides API access, webhooks, and advanced analytics that are unavailable on Free.

### Upgrade from Pro to Enterprise

To upgrade from Pro to Enterprise:

1. Open the subscription or billing settings.
2. Select **Enterprise** or contact FlowDesk Support if an Enterprise agreement is required.
3. Review the Enterprise plan capabilities and applicable terms.
4. Confirm the subscription change.
5. Verify the updated subscription status.

Enterprise adds unlimited agents and tickets, SSO, and SLA to the capabilities available on Pro.

**Note:** Specific pricing and billing adjustments for a plan change are not defined by this policy.

## 9. Downgrading a Subscription

Customers may request a move to a lower subscription tier where the target plan supports their current usage and requirements.

Before downgrading, review the limits and feature availability of the target plan.

For example, moving from Pro to Free would mean losing access to:

- API access.
- Webhooks.
- Advanced analytics.

A downgrade to Free also changes the applicable limits to a maximum of 3 agents and 1,000 tickets per month.

Enterprise customers considering a downgrade should review their contractual terms and contact FlowDesk Support when necessary.

**Warning:** Before downgrading, make sure your account usage is compatible with the limits of the target plan.

## 10. Cancellation

Customers can cancel their subscription through the FlowDesk subscription or billing settings.

### General Cancellation Procedure

1. Open the account's subscription or billing settings.
2. Review the current subscription and billing information.
3. Select the cancellation option.
4. Confirm the cancellation.
5. Verify that the subscription status reflects the cancellation.

Customers should review any applicable subscription or contractual terms before cancelling.

After account termination, FlowDesk retains customer support data for **12 months** in accordance with the data retention policy.

## 11. Subscription Status

Customers can check their current subscription status from the account's subscription or billing settings.

The subscription information should identify the account's current plan, such as:

- Free
- Pro
- Enterprise

Checking the subscription status is useful before:

- Changing plans.
- Reviewing available features.
- Checking applicable usage limits.
- Cancelling a subscription.

## 12. Billing and Renewal

Pro and Enterprise subscriptions are recurring monthly subscriptions.

Customers should expect their subscription to renew according to the applicable monthly billing cycle while the subscription remains active.

Renewal payments are generally non-refundable under the FlowDesk Refund Policy. Exceptions may be approved by FlowDesk Support.

The 14-day refund window applies to the **initial subscription purchase**, not automatically to subsequent renewal payments.

Plan features and limits determine which FlowDesk capabilities are available to an account.

| Capability                |  Free |    Pro | Enterprise |
| ------------------------- | ----: | -----: | ---------: |
| Maximum agents            |     3 |     20 |  Unlimited |
| Maximum tickets per month | 1,000 | 10,000 |  Unlimited |
| API access                |    No |    Yes |        Yes |

| Advanced analytics        |    No |    Yes |        Yes |

### API Access

API access is available on Pro and Enterprise. Free customers cannot access the FlowDesk API.

### Webhooks

Webhooks are available on Pro and Enterprise. They are unavailable on Free.

### Advanced Analytics

Advanced analytics are available on Pro and Enterprise. They are unavailable on Free.

### SSO

SSO is available **only on Enterprise**.

### SLA

SLA is available **only on Enterprise**.

**Note:** Core FlowDesk capabilities such as ticket management, customer management, automation, and standard analytics are part of the product. Specific plan restrictions should be checked against the plan comparison above.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### What plans are available?

FlowDesk offers three subscription tiers:

- Free
- Pro
- Enterprise

### Does Free include API access?

No. API access is unavailable on the Free plan. API access is available on Pro and Enterprise.

### Can I upgrade from Free to Pro?

Yes. Customers can upgrade from Free to Pro through the account's subscription or billing settings.

### Does Enterprise include SSO?

Yes. SSO is available on Enterprise and is not available on Free or Pro.

### Are Pro and Enterprise billed monthly?

Yes. Both Pro and Enterprise customers are billed monthly.

### What happens when I cancel?

The subscription is cancelled according to the applicable cancellation process and terms. After account termination, FlowDesk retains customer support data for 12 months.

### Can I downgrade from Enterprise?

Customers may request a downgrade where applicable. Enterprise customers should review their contractual terms and confirm that their usage and requirements are compatible with the target plan.

### What happens if I downgrade from Pro to Free?

The account becomes subject to Free plan limits and feature availability. API access, webhooks, and advanced analytics are unavailable on Free, and the maximum limits become 3 agents and 1,000 tickets per month.

### Does FlowDesk offer annual billing?

This policy specifies monthly billing for Pro and Enterprise. It does not specify annual billing.

### Does changing plans guarantee a prorated credit?

No specific prorated billing percentage or credit is defined by this policy. Customers should contact FlowDesk Support for questions about the billing treatment of a particular plan change.

### Where can I check my current plan?

You can check your current subscription status from your FlowDesk account's subscription or billing settings.
